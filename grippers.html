The problem

A small ROV needs a gripper that is compact, light and reliable underwater, and the competition vehicle needed an arm that could reach and grasp objects.

What I did

I designed a gear-driven gripper with miniaturised kinematic linkages as an end effector for delicate manipulation on a mini ROV, then printed it to validate the mechanism.

For MATE ROV 2022, I engineered a pneumatic arm on the existing ROV frame: one linear cylinder drives a two-link elbow mechanism and a second cylinder closes the gripper jaws.

The result

A printed gripper prototype and a pneumatic arm integrated on the ROV frame, part of the vehicle that qualified for the MATE ROV World Championship 2022.
